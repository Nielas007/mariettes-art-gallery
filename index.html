Studio

Paintings you can touch with light

Mariëtte builds her paintings up rather than painting them flat. She spreads, combs and carves plaster and texture paste by hand until the surface becomes a landscape of ridges and hollows.

Because the works are almost entirely white, light does the colouring. A morning window, an evening lamp or an LED behind the panel each draw the same piece differently.

Every work is one of a kind. Commissions in your own size and palette are welcome.
